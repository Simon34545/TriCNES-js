to encode the dumps generated by the video dumper:

lossless:
ffmpeg -r 60.0988 -i sc_%06d.png -i audio.wav -vf "scale=iw*1:ih*1" -c:v libx265 -x265-params lossless=1 -c:a flac -pix_fmt yuv420p encoded.mp4

lossy:
ffmpeg -r 60.0988 -i sc_%06d.png -i audio.wav -vf "scale=iw*1:ih*1" -c:v libx264 -crf 18 -c:a libopus -b:a 128k -pix_fmt yuv420p encoded.mp4


change the scaling coefficients to suit your needs
video captured with NTSC decoding on should be in 8:7 aspect ratio (i.e. iw*1:ih*7)
